This review distinguishes supplied interview assertions from independently checked evidence. The incoming document is retained in history/interview_review_2026-10-01/.

## What is worth keeping

The stronger zero-shot and few-shot baselines directly test whether SFT is necessary. Expanded missing-input probes can test the relabel hypothesis more convincingly than seven natural Q5 questions alone. Reporting the cost/quality tradeoff of NF4 versus BF16 is valuable, but should be staged. Fixed prospective checkpoints and reporting seed variability reduce hidden selection. Acknowledging that matching a tool result does not prove causal reliance on that result is appropriate. Keep tool-result tampering, MiniCheck and the data-size curve as deferred work in this round; the supplied document describes them as intentionally deferred, not completed.

## Corrections that change the next experiments

1. **P1 has 40 initial candidates, not approximately 55.** Canonical validation has 62 tool_call records: 47 BMI and 15 unit_convert. Seven BMI records are Q5, leaving 40 grounded BMI cases under the existing check. Removing both measurements can create at most 40 distinct source-record probe pairs before quality exclusions. These are synthetic validation stress tests, not new independent clinical data. Multiple edits/seeds per source do not multiply independent sample size. Keep original answerable partners to detect blanket refusal. Even 0/40 failures has a two-sided 95% Wilson upper limit about 8.8%; a point estimate below 5% cannot establish population risk below 5%.

2. **Calibration needs an event definition.** Mean final-answer token logprob is a ranking score, not P(answer correct). Exponentiating it gives a geometric-mean token probability, which is still not a correctness probability. Report AUROC/risk-coverage against audited labels without calling this calibration. If a probability mapping is fitted, use question-grouped calibration/assessment separation or cross-fitting, disclose repeated validation exposure and bin counts, and do not fit and assess on the same outputs. With only 38 uncertainty questions, ten-bin ECE is unstable. First-sentence token scores need exact token/text alignment, not proportional slicing.

3. **Call-prefix probability is not identical to eventual call probability.** The recorded value concerns the first generated token. It is an interpretable event only under a verified immediate-call grammar and tokenizer; prefaced calls require a different diagnostic. Compute Brier/ECE only with this interpretation stated and compare with observed eventual calls. Report both annotated tool labels and input-grounded policy labels. Validation recalculation: raw1e4 step125 AUC is approximately 0.99983 against annotated type but 0.99664 against the policy excluding Q5. Filtered1e4 epoch two is 0.94235 against annotated type and 1.0 against that policy, despite unresolved free-text behavior. Perfect ranking does not certify safe final answers or threshold decisions.

4. **Scorer limitations are known defects, not only generic caveats.** V2.1 passes a missing or wrong medication and swapped recall counts in executed probes. Its source includes gold-dependent text fallback, canonical external ranges and incomplete contracts. Existing LLM-rater agreement and an unrecorded human spot-check cannot neutralize those defects. Keep v1/v2.1 frozen as diagnostics, add independently checked question obligations and visible-claim factuality before selecting interventions. Do not describe the historical test-derived packets as a clean clinical holdout or repeat the unsupported 0.7 pp bias estimate.

5. **Train fit cannot diagnose rank by itself.** A stratified 200-row subset has only about 40 numeric examples; score them with a validated rubric and record sampling IDs. Errors can arise from labels, decoding, task ambiguity, optimization or capacity. Low train accuracy is not proof rank 16 is too small. High train accuracy is not proof rank 64 must overfit. Use fit as a triage signal and A-R64 as a hypothesis test after checking failures, not an automatic threshold rule.

6. **The BF16 comparison has two factors.** Base BF16 versus base NF4 measures inference quantization. BF16-trained/BF16-served versus NF4-trained/NF4-served changes both training and serving and estimates a recipe difference. To isolate training effects, evaluate both adapters on the same serving precision; ideally complete the 2x2 training-precision by serving-precision crossing after compatibility tests. Check trainable parameter dtypes, frozen modules, layernorm/embedding casts and checkpointing: the k-bit preparation path differs from the nonquantized path. The roughly 13 GB BF16 fit is an estimate, not a demonstrated capacity guarantee. Record peak allocated and reserved memory plus retry/OOM events on longest sequences.

7. **8B is a model comparison, not a capacity proof.** The official Qwen3-8B configuration confirms 36 layers, hidden size 4096, FFN 12288, 8 KV heads and untied embeddings; its card supports hybrid thinking. The official 4B-Instruct-2507 card is non-thinking-only. Use an 8B zero-shot comparator before investing in 8B SFT. Use each model's pinned native template and audit masks/stop tokens. Different post-training, parameter count, template and fixed LR mean an 8B win does not isolate capacity; an 8B loss does not prove data/format is the sole bottleneck. [8B config](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-8B/blob/main/config.json), [8B card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-8B), [4B card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507). Sources inspected 2026-10-01; pin immutable revisions before runs.

8. **BF16/QLoRA memory arithmetic is only a budget.** Context, workspaces, optimizer dtype and activation peaks vary. GB and GiB must be distinguished. Full fine-tuning memory depends on optimizer/precision implementation; do not present the 64 GB subtotal as a universal exact requirement. Similar early minibatch losses do not prove gradient-normalization equivalence. A synthetic variable-length, identical-effective-batch gradient/update comparison with dropout controlled is the relevant test; attention-kernel selection requires profiling rather than inference from batch size alone.

9. **Statistical power is paired and endpoint-specific.** The independent two-proportion approximation is not a hard minimum detectable difference for these paired outputs, and a macro is not a single binomial proportion with n=250. Report discordant pairs, exact paired tests where appropriate, and type-stratified paired intervals. A CI containing zero is inconclusive, not equivalence. Plan power over plausible discordance rates; do not replace this with seed SD. Three seeds characterize some training variation but not all scorer/question uncertainty. Repeated outputs on seven Q5 records must be clustered by question.

10. **Several engineering claims are stale or incomplete.** Git history exists: current HEAD at review was c4f3e0d, preceded by c03023c and 3142385. The current requirements.txt really does contain only CPU/dev dependencies; the authoritative training route is the locked train extra, and the export/documentation should be reconciled before shipping. Do not delete or merge ClinicalQA blindly; no divergence audit was performed here. Exact tokenizer shares, few-shot sequence lengths and first-sentence alignment remain work items. Training token shares do not measure gradient influence. Strong extraction scores from a known incomplete scorer cannot establish table serialization is solved.

## Revised hypothesis hierarchy

Primary: audited Q5 relabeling improves missing-input behavior without damaging valid calls, versus filtered training at the same stated recipe. Secondary: SFT improves on a fixed strong zero-/few-shot baseline sufficiently to justify its runtime or controllability tradeoff. Mechanism exploration: numeric explanation and implicit-allergy augmentation. Optional engineering: quantization, model size and rank. This prioritizes useful conclusions over answering every interview question with another GPU run.

The supplied report's fabrication totals differ from earlier documents (18 versus 17 text cases and some cohort denominators). Freeze an explicit run roster and item-level labeling rule before quoting a single pooled number. This review does not claim to reproduce those aggregate fabrication labels or the informal human check. No new GPU work was launched.
