## Verified wave-two evidence

All 14 v2.1 score files contain 250 records, and per-type counts match summary.json (3,500 scored rows). The completed three filtered training manifests record 242 optimizer steps, approximately 2,560-2,565 seconds of training and 7.81 GiB peak allocated memory. Micro-batch 1 and accumulation 16 are appropriate measured defaults on this RTX 4090; speeds on other GPU types or batch 8 have not been established.

Prompt v2 does not show a consistent numeric gain, but the claim that each category flips only 0-2 answers is inaccurate. On base, v2.1 records extractive 93 to 92 (five flips), numeric 41 to 41 (four flips), tool 39 to 46 (13 flips), uncertainty 23 to 23 (four flips). Counts of net gains are different from counts of changed labels. Filtered5e5 goes from 41 to 42 numeric and 60 to 62 tool passes. These are provisional scorer-specific outcomes, not adjudicated truth. Retaining v1 for simplicity is defensible; stating v2 was proven ineffective is not. The external-range scorer policy is also misaligned with the range-only prompt.

For epoch two, saved v2.1 macros are approximately 0.914 at 1e-4, 0.921 at 1.5e-4 and 0.941 at 2e-4. Recomputed answer-type-stratified paired bootstrap over validation questions (5,000 replicates, seed 42) gives 1.5e-4 minus 1e-4 +0.66 pp, interval [-1.06, +2.79] pp; 2e-4 minus 1e-4 +2.72 pp, interval [+0.35, +5.53] pp. These unadjusted, post-inspection comparisons exclude seed variance and scorer uncertainty and do not justify declaring a winner. They do contradict a blanket assertion that all comparisons lie within statistical noise. A 1e-4 default should be described as conservative and prospectively locked, not uniquely best. Epoch one is actually higher than epoch two at 1e-4 under this scorer, so two epochs is a future protocol choice rather than an established optimum.

## Main methodological corrections

1. **Scorer validity remains the largest release issue.** Hash freezing prevents silent change, not false passes. Wrong medication, omitted question components and swapped numeric roles already passed executed validation probes. Current v2.1 may be reported as a diagnostic baseline but cannot certify the effects of explanation-based training. Gold agreement does not prove a key is complete or input-grounded.
2. **Visible reasoning needs full-response factuality checks.** Answer-only accuracy may be one metric, but false Working text remains user-visible. A-VIS changes numeric targets and imperial tool-turn text together; split these arms or describe the bundled effect. Longer targets also change token weighting and exposure. Call probability after a generated preamble is conditioned on that preamble and is not the same calibrated decision signal as first-token call probability.
3. **Q5 relabeling is conditional.** Audit all candidates before replacing labels. A filtered-to-relabel comparison changes count (1,922 to at most 2,000), class mix and updates (242 to at most 250). It is a policy comparison unless exposure controls are added. A no-call answer can still invent measurements; the proposed in-text fabrication diagnostic is valuable but must distinguish asserted patient facts, hypothetical examples and quoted missing values. It must not label every number in a refusal as fabrication.
4. **Uncertainty is not equivalence.** A confidence interval containing zero does not establish no difference. No-call noninferiority needs a prespecified margin and one-sided bound, not merely failure to find an increase. Three-seed mean/SD is descriptive. Wilson intervals apply to binomial rates, not directly to an unweighted four-type macro. Bootstrap question pairs, stratify types for macro and treat multiple checkpoint outputs on one question as repeated measures. If an ablation gain matters, confirm it across matched seeds.
5. **Consumed test remains exploratory.** Re-running a frozen list is better than selecting on test, but does not restore independence. No evidence supplied supports a 0.7 pp contamination-bias estimate. Preserve the original reports and explicitly label any new test result reused-test exploratory; do not call it a fresh final evaluation. The existing final-eval implementation needs an explicit multi-seed/fixed-epoch manifest path while retaining identity/hash checks.
6. **Spec metrics need honest denominators.** The assignment says tool selection is the proportion of tool_call examples choosing the right tool, not only the grounded subset. Show the full annotated denominator, plus the clinically grounded policy diagnostic. Conditional argument accuracy can look high when the model refuses most calls, so also show coverage and end-to-end success. The assignment does not prescribe a universal numeric tolerance.
7. **The mechanistic claims are hypotheses.** Token count share is not gradient contribution. Three LR results do not prove weighting cannot help. Gold choosing relative deviation does not establish a unique meaning of most clinically abnormal across mixed units. Standard reference ranges absent from an input require an explicit task policy; common gold usage alone does not establish correctness. Allergy rephrasing must preserve meaning and avoid swapping one lexical shortcut for another.
8. **Probes need causal consistency.** Removing height requires editing every mention in note/table/question and any BMI that would reveal the answer; adding or converting measurements requires updating related statements. A P4 value edit must recompute tool outputs and derived facts. Specify input-derived expected behavior before inspecting answers. Include A-VIS if interpreting its numeric mechanism. Roughly 40 pairs are stress tests, not population accuracy estimates.

Claims not independently verified here: supervised-token proportions, 17/42 versus 1/28 text fabrication, 36/36 versus 8/18 allergy behavior, 51 versus 5 training templates, and 13/15 ambiguous scale choices. Supply IDs, exact run roster, operational definitions and extraction code; these need the missing findings/evidence. Counts pooled over multiple checkpoints must not be treated as independent patient samples.

## Revised priority

Keep v1, unweighted CE, LR 1e-4 and micro-batch 1 as a simple candidate control; avoid claiming these are uniquely optimal. First settle evaluator policy and review Q5 annotations. Then run the relabeled control, check full-answer/tool safety outcomes, and only then spend on the extra seeds. Add the stronger prompted baseline early to quantify the value of SFT. Treat A-VIS/A-IMPL as separate exploratory ablations, with explicit context-preserving transformations and full-response checks. No need to implement every optional extension before obtaining the central relabel result.
